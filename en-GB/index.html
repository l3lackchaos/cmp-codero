About Us

A Family Business Built on Reputation

L+P Mullen Construction Ltd is a family-run building contractor based in Doncaster, South Yorkshire. Brothers Lee and Paul Mullen bring over fifty years' combined experience to every project and in that time, they have built a reputation founded entirely on the quality of their work and the trust of the people they work for.

Lee and Paul lead every project personally, from the first site visit through to handover. For structural and general construction works, we have our own experienced team. Where specialist skills are called for plastering, electrics, plumbing, joinery and the like we bring in trusted tradespeople we have worked closely alongside for many years. These are craftspeople whose quality we know inside out and are proud to put our name to.

Our diary is kept busy almost entirely through referrals from previous clients. That's the only measure of quality that really matters to us.
