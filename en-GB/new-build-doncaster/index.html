Bespoke New Build Homes in Doncaster & South Yorkshire

Building a new home is one of the most significant projects you will ever undertake. At L+P Mullen, we bring over fifty years' combined experience to every new build combining traditional construction techniques with contemporary design to create homes that are built to last and tailored entirely to your brief.

Based in Doncaster and working across South Yorkshire, we specialise in one-off bespoke new builds for private clients. Every project gets the personal attention of Lee and Paul Mullen directly from the first site visit through to handing over the keys.

Bespoke Homes Built to Your Specification

No two builds are the same. We work closely with you and your architect (or our own design contacts) to understand exactly what you want, then manage the entire construction process. From groundworks and structural concrete to full fit-out including kitchens, bathrooms, and landscaping we handle it all.

Small Developments & Plots

As well as single new build homes, we take on small residential developments and plot builds across Doncaster and South Yorkshire. If you have land and need a reliable, experienced contractor to deliver to a high specification and on budget, we're happy to discuss your plans.

Why L+P Mullen?

We are Builders of Excellence Award nominees, and a family business whose reputation has been built entirely through repeat clients and personal referrals. That track record matters when you're commissioning a new home.

  • Bespoke single new build homes
  • Small residential developments
  • Barn conversions and luxury apartment conversions
  • Structural concrete, blockwork and groundworks
  • Full fit-out including kitchens, bathrooms and landscaping
  • Liaison with architects and planning authorities
