Aboard MV Sea Gipsy

Small ship. Immense archipelago.

The Mergui Archipelago is a scattering of more than 700 islands in southern Myanmar. Aboard Sea Gipsy, the journey moves at island pace: snorkelling reefs, kayaking mangroves and anchoring far from crowded routes.
