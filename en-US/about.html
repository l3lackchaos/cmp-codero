About Island Safaris

Island Safaris

Small-group island journeys

Explore the Mergui Archipelago with Island Safaris, using MV Sea Gipsy as your base between island stops. Days combine cruising with opportunities to swim, snorkel, paddle and spend time ashore. Return aboard for meals and nights at anchor, without changing hotels. Choose southern island bays or the Lampi mangrove route, depending on the itinerary you select.

Our operator, Moby Dick Tours, has offered boat charters since 2002. Our programmes start in Kawthaung, southern Myanmar, and range from four to ten days. You can enquire about joining a shared journey or charter Sea Gipsy for your own group, depending on what best fits your plans.

Small groups on the water

Sea Gipsy carries up to ten guests, with shared departures normally requiring at least four. This makes the experience ideal for couples and friends who enjoy travelling with a small group. You do not need to hire the entire vessel to enquire about a trip. Browse the available dates or tell us when you would like to travel, and we can discuss how your plans may fit with other enquiries.

A boat with its own character

Life aboard is informal, with curtained sleeping gazebos, shared bathrooms and communal meals. The gazebos are open-air spaces with double beds and small fans, rather than enclosed, air-conditioned cabins. Between activities, the decks offer places to sit, watch the coastline, and spend time with other guests. The boat photographs and film offer a closer look at the setting before you book.

Different routes for different journeys

Our four-day and five-day programmes explore the southern islands, including Cock's Comb and Horse Shoe Island. The six-day route brings in the Lampi mangroves and Kyun Pila, while the ten-day programme covers a wider circuit before returning south. Each package includes an arrival hotel night, followed by a different number of nights aboard. Compare the daily itineraries to find the places and length of stay that appeal to you.

Your own group aboard

For friends, families or couples planning together, a private charter offers exclusive use of Sea Gipsy. Start with your group size, preferred dates and interests, then discuss a practical programme with us. Accommodation and facilities remain as shown on the boat page, while the route depends on available time, current access, and conditions. Your quotation will explain the agreed arrangements and costs.

Visiting with care

The archipelago is both a natural environment and a place where people live and work. During shore visits, follow local guidance, respect private spaces and ask before photographing people. In the water, avoid touching coral or collecting natural material. Activities, wildlife encounters and village visits depend on the route and what is possible on the day. Taking care at each stop is part of travelling thoughtfully through the islands.

Plan with us

An enquiry lets us understand the journey you have in mind. Please tell us your dates, number of travellers and the places you most want to see. We will check availability, discuss accommodation and provide a complete quotation before you book. Review our practical planning information and current official travel advice alongside those arrangements. When you are ready to explore the options, browse the itineraries or contact us with your plans.
