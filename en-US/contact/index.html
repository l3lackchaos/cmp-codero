Plan your arrival in Kawthaung

Find a shared departure

If you have seen a date that suits you, include it in your message along with your preferred itinerary and the number of people travelling. Couples can enquire for two places without organising another couple themselves. We will check availability for your preferred dates and explain the booking arrangements. Please contact us directly for the latest information about the journey you are considering.

If you are still comparing routes, please tell us what interests you most. A short southern circuit, the Lampi mangroves and a longer journey through different island groups each point towards different itineraries. Knowing your priorities helps us discuss options that fit your time.

Tell us your travel window

Let us know your earliest possible arrival and latest return, as well as your preferred trip duration. Mention any fixed flights or dates you cannot change. If you can travel a little earlier or later, include that flexibility too. This helps us discuss a shared departure alongside other enquiries without assuming everyone's plans are the same.

Plan a private charter

For exclusive boat hire, send your group size, preferred dates and the number of nights you would like aboard. Include children's ages where relevant, together with any particular places or activities your group is interested in. We can review availability and a practical route before providing a quotation. Explore the Sea Gipsy accommodation photographs with your party so everyone understands the open-air gazebos and shared facilities.

Ask about the details that matter

If you have questions about sleeping arrangements, dietary needs, or moving around the boat, ask when you enquire. Solo travellers can ask about sharing options and gazebo supplements. For arrival planning, tell us whether you expect to travel via Kawthaung or Ranong. We can review the transfer arrangements, while you check current official travel advice, entry requirements and insurance for your journey.

Keep your first message simple

Include your name, email address, guest count, possible dates and preferred itinerary if you have chosen one. Add a contact number if you would like to speak to us, plus any questions you would like answered. There is no need to send payment details or passport scans with a general enquiry. We can explain any document requirements separately as your plans progress.

From enquiry to booking

Before proceeding, you will need a complete quotation, written confirmation of availability and the applicable payment and cancellation terms. An enquiry does not reserve a sleeping space or confirm your booking. Your booking is confirmed by email once accepted and the required payment has been received. If you are already booked, include your booking reference when contacting us so we can connect your question with your agreed journey.
